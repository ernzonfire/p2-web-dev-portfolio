03 / Behind the work

A creative eye.
A practical mindset.

I’m Ernie, a video editor and creative based in Cebu, exploring how thoughtful design and technology can make everyday things work better.

My background in video production shapes how I approach pacing, visual hierarchy, and the details that make a story feel right. I bring that same care to digital products.

For web and app development, I use AI tools to help write, review, and troubleshoot code while I build my technical foundation. I focus on shaping the experience, testing the important flows, and iterating on what people actually need.
